# Commercial Licensing

**Most people do not need this page.** If you are running Self-Hosted AI Chat on
your own servers — for yourself, your clients, or your company — the
[AGPL-3.0](LICENSE) already covers you, at no cost, with no conditions to meet.
Stop reading and go install it.

## When you do need it

The AGPL has one requirement that matters here: if you modify this project and
let other people use it over a network, you must offer those users the source of
your modified version.

That is fine for most self-hosters. It is a genuine problem if you want to:

- Run a hosted AI chat product built on a modified version without publishing
  your modifications
- Bundle this project inside a proprietary product you ship to customers
- Build on it under a company policy that forbids AGPL code

A commercial licence removes the source-sharing obligation. Everything else
about the software is identical — same code, same repository, same releases.

## What a commercial licence does and does not cover

Being straight with you about the scope, because this project has a history:

- It covers the modifications and additions authored by Karan Garg and by
  contributors who have signed the [CLA](CLA.md).
- It does **not** need to cover the portions derived from
  [Vercel's AI Chatbot template](https://github.com/vercel/ai-chatbot). Those are
  Apache-2.0 and copyright 2024 Vercel, Inc. — you already have permissive
  rights to them, from Vercel, for free, whether or not you buy anything here.
  See [NOTICE](NOTICE).

In other words, you are paying for a licence to the work built on top, not for
access to something that was already open. Anyone claiming otherwise would be
selling you something they do not own.
